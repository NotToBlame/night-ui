🌙 Night UI is a minimalistic dark-themed resource pack designed to be easy on the eyes while maintaining a clean and modern look. Whether you're playing late at night or simply prefer a darker aesthetic, this pack enhances your UI experience without unnecessary clutter.

## ✨ Features  
- ✅**Lightweight & Minimalistic** – Low resolution textures to keep impact low  
- ✅**Dark Theme for Comfort** – Reduce eye strain and enhance readability.  
- ✅**Compatible with Other Resource Packs** – Works seamlessly alongside other packs for full customization.

- [Simple Voice Chat](https://modrinth.com/plugin/simple-voice-chat)
